Tea Room (With Hedgehog)

Houzhi Ke
2023-2024
Acrylic paint, watercolor, crayon, colored pencil

This room next to the Fish and Elephant is a warm, orange Tea Room. Here, the central character shares a quiet moment with a hedgehog. This room reflects imagination as a coping mechanism.

The cozy colors and gentle scene offer a possible pause, a way to reframe emotional tension. Visitors are invited to interpret this room in their own way: as comfort, escape, grounding, or something in between.
