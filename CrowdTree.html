Crowd/Tree

Houzhi Ke
2023-2024
Acrylic paint, clay

Upon entering, a busy crowd scene sets an overwhelming tone. Yet upon looking up, the two part painting transitions into a contrastly serene environment. Here the artist presents a sharp showcase of how the outer world can impact a person's inner senses.
