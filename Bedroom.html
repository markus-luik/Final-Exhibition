Bedroom

Houzhi Ke
2023-2024
Acrylic paint

Upon entering the house, you land in the Bedroom. The room is constructed from layered visual elements that reflect several internal pressures at once: hesitation, difficulty initiating action, social anxiety, and the feeling of being overwhelmed.

This space is distincly personal, allowing a look into the artist world.

Note: From here, you can choose to move left or right, beginning your exploration of the remaining rooms.
