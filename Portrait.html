Portrait

Houzhi Ke
2023-2024
Acrylic paint, DVD fragments, glass fragments, fluffy balls, color pencil

The last artwork is a portrait using both cool and warm tones, a moment where emotions settle into the same space. After moving through tension, fear, and coping, this piece offers a point of coexistence rather than resolution.
