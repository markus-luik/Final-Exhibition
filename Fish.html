The Living Room (Giant Fish)

Houzhi Ke
2023-2024
Acrylic paint

Here, to left of the bedroom, is the Living Room depicting a startling scene of a giant fish breaking through the door. This room represents fear – a disruptive force that blocks retreat and demands confrontation.

Note: Flashing lights warning!

Note: Once you enter this room, you cannot return to the Bedroom; you must face what’s ahead to continue forward.
