Visitor Guide

Welcome to the digital house.

This exhibition is designed as a linear journey through a series of emotional spaces. Visitors begin outside and gradually move inward, encountering visual environments that reflect different internal states and psychological responses.

There are a total of six artworks. Interact with them using your mouse and read more about them using the about button.

Note: There are flashing lights in one of the artworks.

The Window

Houzhi Ke
2023-2024
Windowframe, acrylic paint, paper cutouts, pill bottles

Your experience begins outside the house. These exterior scenes function as an entry window, setting the tone for the journey ahead. They introduce the shifting sensitivities behind the series – movement, attention, and emotional transition – before guiding you toward the interior rooms.
