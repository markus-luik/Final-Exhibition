Elephant Room

Houzhi Ke
2023-2024
Colored pencil

The room right of the bedroom is filled with elephants.

A large elephant, constructed from nine smaller ones, occupies most of the space. The central character is squeezed between them.

This room illustrates the feeling of being overwhelmed – the sense of being pressed by multiple internal and external demands at once.

Note: The elephants come to life when a cursor hovers over!
